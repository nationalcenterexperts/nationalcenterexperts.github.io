THE COURSE IN ONE VIEW

A national center is not a building. It is a system people choose to build together.

This seminar follows the full arc of institution-building: define a public purpose, design the organization, align partners and resources, and create the habits that make the work sustainable.

Participants use cases, guest perspectives, discussion, and a developing class project to turn those questions into a practical framework.

THE INSTITUTION-BUILDING PATH

Four questions guide the semester.

Each part of the course adds another layer to a center that can create national value.

01 · PURPOSE

Why should it exist?

Clarify the mission, the need it answers, and the public value worth organizing around.

02 · PEOPLE

Who must be at the table?

Identify leaders, partners, participants, and communities whose trust makes the work possible.

03 · OPERATIONS

How will the work happen?

Connect governance, funding, responsibilities, communication, and everyday systems.

04 · SUSTAINABILITY

What keeps it useful?

Build evaluation, adaptation, durable relationships, and long-term relevance into the design.

ABOUT THE SEMINAR

Learn how institutions become capable of lasting impact.

What does it take to transform a bold idea into a national center capable of reshaping an entire industry?

RNR 7029 is a collaborative seminar about why major centers, institutions, and organizations exist and how they operate. Together, participants explore how a mission takes shape, partnerships are formed, resources are secured, challenges are addressed, and a visionary concept becomes an influential institution.

ENVISION

Start with purpose.

Explore mission, public value, strategic planning, and the decisions that give an institution direction.

OPERATE

Make the idea work.

Study organizational design, leadership, governance, funding, partnerships, and day-to-day operations.

SUSTAIN

Build for the future.

Consider growth, evaluation, adaptation, and the long-term relationships that keep a center relevant.

THE CENTRAL QUESTION

What does it take to transform a bold idea into a national center capable of reshaping an entire industry?

Students do not simply learn about this process; they step into it. Case studies, guest speakers, and interactive discussions reveal the full lifecycle of institution-building.

The class examines strategic planning, organizational design, leadership, governance, fundraising, partnerships, operations, growth, and long-term sustainability.

LEARNING EXPERIENCE

From visionary idea to influential institution.

Participants engage with founders and administrative teams, contribute broader perspectives, and develop a practical framework for translating visionary ideas into reality.

The course emphasizes entrepreneurship, innovation, collaboration, and transformative leadership.
