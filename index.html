ABOUT THE SEMINAR

Bold ideas can become national institutions.

RNR 7029 takes participants behind the scenes to discover how impactful institutions are envisioned, built, funded, launched, operated, and sustained.

Through case studies, guest speakers, and interactive discussion, the class explores the strategy, leadership, governance, partnerships, and collaboration that can turn a visionary idea into lasting national impact.

THE CENTRAL QUESTION

What does it take to transform a bold idea into a national center capable of reshaping an entire industry?

This seminar explores how impactful institutions are envisioned, built, funded, launched, operated, and sustained.

Participants examine strategic planning, organizational design, leadership, governance, fundraising, partnerships, operations, growth, and long-term sustainability.

LEARNING EXPERIENCE

From visionary idea to influential institution.

Students engage with founders and administrative teams, build a practical framework for institution-building, and contribute through discussion and project work.

The course emphasizes entrepreneurship, innovation, collaboration, and transformative leadership.
